## Regression vs Classification.
Regression is the continuous prediction of value's by a function,while classification is the separation of data points based on some criteria.

## Perceptron.
A single unit of information processing that takes in some inputs ,performs weighing addition and then applies an activation function.

## Decision Bounary.
It is a hyperplane separating different classes of data.

## Activation Functions.
They are functions which activate based on some combination of inputs(the activation condition differs according to different tasks.).eg-sigmoid.

## Matrix Multiplication.
It is a core part of Neural networks as any Deep neural net can be represented as a matrix multiplication of inputs and weights followed by an activation function.

## Quantum State Vectors.
Mathematical description's of quantum information.
