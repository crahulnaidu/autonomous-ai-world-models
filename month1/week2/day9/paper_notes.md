## Paper Title=Deep Sparse Rectifier Neural Networks — Glorot, Bordes & Bengio (2011).


The paper explores Rectified linear unit as an activation function instead of logistic sigmoid and tanh for deep neural networks. It tries to emulate biological neurons which are sparsely activated at any given time.
Not only is ReLU computationally efficient than sigmoid and tanh ,it also performs better on some image classification tasks on datasets like MNIST,CIFAR-10, than pre-trained unsupervised models with sigmoid and tanh.
