## Optimization Review
Optimization in ML is the process of adjusting the model parameters to reduce the loss function.There are different types of optimizers that help with this depending upon the model like-gradient descent,momentum,adam.etc

## Learning Curves.
They are plot's of loss vs iterations,parameter's vs iterations,gradient norm vs iterations.They are used to check how the model behave's with different iterations.

## Gradient Norm.
It is the norm of the gradient vector  of the cost function.It is used to calculate the direction of steepest ascent for a function.

## Vectorization.
It is the process of turning a normal python list into a numpy array for faster vector operations.

## Quantum Review.
Qubits are represented in dirac notation as |0> and |1>.Any quantum state vector is written as the superposition of basis states such that the euclidian norm is equal to one.Unitary matrices/gates are operations on quantum state vectors which are rotations on the bloch sphere.Bell states are a set of 4 entangled states that form the basis for all 2 qubit state vectors.A H gate create's a superposition from an ordinary |0> or |1> state by 90 degree rotation along the Y axis.
